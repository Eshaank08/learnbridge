# LearnBridge — Vision

## What are we building?
A platform where any student anywhere types a question in any language and instantly gets a free, expert-taught lesson — funded by sponsor companies who get brand visibility in return.

## Who is it for?
Primary: Students in low-income or non-English-speaking contexts who can't afford tutors or whose language isn't served by existing platforms.
Secondary: Teachers who want global reach without building a business. Sponsors with CSR budgets who want credible student reach.

## Why does it matter?
Khan Academy exists. Coursera exists. The gap: they're in English, they're passive (no AI follow-up), and they have no sustainable funding model that doesn't require charging students. LearnBridge closes all three gaps simultaneously.

## Core Features (MVP only)
1. Natural language search — student types anything, in any language
2. AI match — Claude maps query → subject → concept → lesson card (top 3)
3. Lesson view — mock video + sponsor badge + follow-up Q&A via Claude
4. Follow-up Q&A — Claude answers anchored strictly to the lesson content

## MVP Scope (hackathon day)
- Search input page
- AI match (Claude API, live)
- 3-card results UI
- Single lesson view with sponsor badge
- Follow-up Q&A chat (live Claude, scoped to lesson)
- Teacher mini-profile on lesson card
- 20+ mock lessons across 4 subjects in seed data
- Hindi demo flow works end-to-end

## Out of scope for hackathon
- Teacher onboarding
- Real video hosting
- Sponsor payment/billing
- Auth / accounts
- Mobile
