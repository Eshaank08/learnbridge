# LearnBridge — Competitive Landscape

- Khan Academy: free, structured, trusted. Weakness: English-first, passive consumption, no AI Q&A, no sponsor model for makers outside the US.
- Byju's: adaptive learning, huge brand in India. Weakness: premium paywall, India-centric, no multilingual AI tutor for low-income learners.
- Coursera: university quality, global scale. Weakness: certificate-driven, expensive, English-dominant, not built for basic-secondary remediation.
- YouTube Education: infinite content, zero cost. Weakness: no structure, no skill mapping, no follow-up, no guaranteed accuracy, no offline sponsor sustainability.

- Gap: no platform combines multilingual input + AI-scoped follow-up + sponsor-funded free access for underserved students.
- Gap: teachers from the Global South have no lightweight path to monetize expertise globally.
- Gap: CSR budgets want measurable student reach, but education sponsorship is fragmented and undigital.

- Differentiator: AI tutor speaks your language and stays on the lesson, sponsor-funded so students pay zero.
- Differentiator: one question maps to a complete lesson, not search results, not a 12-minute generic video.
- Differentiator: teacher-led, not platform-created, so content scales without rising marginal cost.

- Problem scale: UNESCO estimates 244 million children and youth are out of school globally, and half of students in low-income countries cannot read a simple sentence by age 10.

- Judge one-liner: Khan Academy teaches in English. LearnBridge teaches in the student\u2019s language and funds it through sponsors.
