# LearnBridge — User Journeys

## Student Journey
- Lands on learnbridge.xyz.
- Sees one search bar and a tagline.
- Types a question in any language: "मुझे photosynthesis समझाओ".
- Sees 3 lesson cards: teacher name, country flag, subject, duration, sponsor name.
- Clicks a card.
- Watches the lesson video placeholder.
- Sees sponsor bar: "This lesson is free thanks to Bayer. Investing in the scientists of tomorrow."
- Asks a follow-up question.
- Gets an answer only from the lesson content. Zero paywall. Zero signup.

## Teacher Journey
- Creates a lesson summary for a topic they know.
- Enters credentials and context once.
- Lesson is published.
- Gets paid per lesson view through sponsor revenue share.

## Sponsor Journey
- Targets specific subjects: Biology, Physics, Mathematics, History.
- Brand appears in lesson metadata and sponsor bar.
- Gets impression tracking and student reach reporting.
- Funds free access for students as CSR or talent pipeline investment.
