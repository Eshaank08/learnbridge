# LearnBridge — Pitch Prep

## Business model
- CPM estimate for education audience: EUR 3\u20138 per 1,000 lesson views
- 100K monthly student views \u00d7 EUR 5 CPM \u00d7 10 sponsors = EUR 50,000/month at scale
- Compare: European education inventory on programmatic exchanges trades at 2\u20136 EUR CPM
- Teachers paid through a rev-share pool, fixed minimum guarantee for onboarding
- Gross margin high: marginal cost of an additional lesson view is near zero

## Judge Q&A

### How is this different from Khan Academy?
Khan Academy is English-first and static. LearnBridge matches a student\u2019s exact question to a lesson, then adds a scoped AI tutor in their language, all funded by sponsors.

### Why sponsor instead of ads?
Sponsors get brand visibility inside lesson units, measurable reach metrics, and CSR impact reporting. They reach students and parents while funding access directly.

### What stops a big tech company from copying this?
Distribution with real teacher partners in global south markets and the learner trust loop. Copying the UI is easy. Building trusted teacher network and local-language curriculum is not.

### How do you ensure content quality?
Lessons are teacher-created with structured summaries. Claude is scoped to lesson text only, so answers can never go off-topic. Sponsor does not alter content.

### What\u2019s the business model long term?
Tiered: student tier is free, sponsor-funded. Multi-country expansion increases CPM. Intermediate layer adds verified certification and employer connects.
