# LearnBridge — Business Model

## Revenue model
- CPM sponsorship model.
- Target rate: EUR 5-10 per 1,000 lesson views for education audience.
- Sponsors buy category exclusivity or targeted placement.

## Unit economics
- 1 lesson view = 1 sponsorship impression.
- 10 sponsors × EUR 5 CPM × 100K monthly views = EUR 50,000/month.
- European programmatic education inventory benchmarks at EUR 2-6 CPM.
- Variable cost per view is near zero.

## Teacher payout
- Fixed minimum guarantee for onboarding: EUR 25-75 per lesson.
- Ongoing rev-share from sponsor pool per view.
- Top-performing teachers earn more views = more revenue.

## Go-to-market
1. Launch with 4 subjects, 20 lessons, English + Hindi + Swahili + German.
2. Onboard 10 anchor sponsors focused on STEM and humanities CSR.
3. Distribute through teacher networks and social channels.
4. Convert sponsor spend from CSR and employer branding budgets.

## Competitive advantage
- Content is teacher-created, not platform-produced: low marginal cost.
- Sponsors fund access directly: no subscription fatigue.
- Multilingual AI matching removes geography and language friction.
